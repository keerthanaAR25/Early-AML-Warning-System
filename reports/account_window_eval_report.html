How to read this. The forecast horizon H changes the task, so every horizon is reported. H=2 is the dashboard task. The gains at H=7 and H=14 come mainly from a better-posed task and protocol (more positives, threshold chosen on validation, purge gaps), not from a better model: account-history features do not help consistently. The PR-AUC range resamples whole accounts; it is NOT a confidence interval (with few positive accounts it is skewed and can sit above the estimate). Wilson intervals are for the operating point and are approximate because rows of one account are correlated. ROC-AUC is omitted from the table because the active-accounts-only label inflates it.
